More Facts About Polar Bears

  • Polar bears are endangered due to habitat loss from global warming.
  • Are considered as marine mammals (just like dolphins)
  • The skin under thier fur is black.
  • Can swim up to 6mph.
  • They can swim for days.
  • Unlike other bears, they do not hibernate.
  • Their paws measure up to 30 centimeters.
  • Largest polar bear recroded weighed 2,209 pounds and was nearly 12ft tall.
